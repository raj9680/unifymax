Why a loop, not a stack
Break one link and the loop stops.

A bot without governed knowledge guesses. Knowledge nobody scores goes stale. Training without quality data trains the wrong thing. Agents without AI beside them are slow. Each capability exists to feed the next, which is why Unifymax is sold as one platform and measured as one outcome.

Outcome-based, not effort-based

You don't buy seats. You don't buy hours. You buy the outcome.
